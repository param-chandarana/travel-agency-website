Frequently Asked Questions

3. How do I book a tour or vacation package?

Booking with us is easy! You can either:

  • Contact us directly through phone or email to speak with one of our travel experts.
  • Visit our website and use our user-friendly enquiry system and we will get back to you.
4. Are there any discounts available for group bookings?

Yes, we offer special discounts for group bookings. The specific discount amount may vary depending on the size of your group and the chosen destination. Contact us for more details.

5. Can I make changes to my booking after it's confirmed?

We understand that plans can change. Depending on the type of booking and availability, we may be able to accommodate changes. Please contact us as soon as possible to discuss your request.

6. What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept various forms of payment, including credit cards, debit cards, UPI, NEFT, RTGS, cheques, and demand drafts. Our payment process is designed to be convenient and secure for our customers.

7. Are there any hidden fees or extra charges in your packages?

No, we are committed to transparency. Our package prices include all the essential components mentioned in the itinerary. Any optional extras or additional services will be clearly communicated to you during the booking process.

8. Is travel insurance included in the packages?

Yes, travel insurance up to 59 years of age included in our packages unless mentioned otherwise.

9. Can you provide information about the safety of my chosen destination?

We prioritize the safety of our customers. Before offering packages to any destination, we thoroughly assess the safety and security situation. We stay updated on travel advisories and provide relevant information to our customers. However, it's essential for travellers to stay informed and follow safety guidelines during their trip.

10. Do you offer both domestic and international packages?

Yes, we offer packages to a wide range of destinations, both in India and across the globe. You can explore our website or contact us for more information about the packages available for your desired location.

11. What travel documents do I need for international travel?

The required travel documents, such as passports, visas, and vaccinations, vary depending on your destination and nationality. We provide guidance on the necessary documentation for your specific trip. It's essential to ensure that your documents are up to date well in advance of your departure.

12. How can I provide feedback or write a review about my travel experience?

We value your feedback! You can share your travel experiences and reviews through our website or on popular review platforms. You can also email us at travelvista@example.com. Your feedback helps us improve our services and assist future travellers in making informed decisions.
